# Key Insights

1. **Overall:** R$13.18M revenue from 96,211 delivered orders and 93,104 customers (Jan 2017 - Aug 2018). Average order value: R$137.
2. **Growth then a plateau:** Jan-Aug 2018 revenue was R$7.22M, **141% higher** than Jan-Aug 2017 (R$2.99M). But growth stopped in 2018: monthly revenue peaked at R$977.5K in May 2018 and was R$838.6K by Aug 2018 (-14%).
3. **Black Friday:** Nov 2017 revenue jumped **52%** over the month before. The single best day, 24 Nov 2017, brought R$149.9K - 2.5x the next best day.
4. **Categories:** 74 categories sell, but 18 of them make 80% of revenue. The top 3 (Health beauty, Watches gifts, Bed bath table) bring 25.9%.
5. **80/20 rule:** 26% of products (8,310 of 32,081) generate 80% of revenue.
6. **Geography:** SP, RJ, MG bring 63% of revenue (SP alone 38%). Shipping is costliest in PE, where freight equals 23% of the order value (vs 17% overall).
7. **Loyalty:** only **3.0%** of customers ordered more than once, and they bring just 5.5% of revenue. Almost all revenue depends on finding new customers.
8. **Weekdays:** Mon is the best day (16.4% of revenue); Sat is the weakest (11.1%). Weekends sell less than any weekday.
9. **Payments:** credit card pays for 78% of the money, spread over 3.5 instalments on average.
10. **Forecast:** the backtest picked **the naive method (next month = last month)** (average error 9.3%); a straight-line trend was off by 21.1% because growth stopped in 2018. Expected revenue is about **R$838.6K a month** for Sep 2018 - Nov 2018, with a range of R$789.9K-R$887.2K next month widening to R$720.3K-R$956.8K three months out. November will likely beat this because of Black Friday.

_Revenue = item prices of delivered orders; freight is excluded. Amounts in Brazilian reais (R$)._
